# The final prototype's stability run (milestone 7; development build: the pack's size, the alignment, test
# positions, raised Health so the fight never ends in death). About 30 minutes of the completed fight: four evil
# wolves taking turns, Ice Walls rising and melting among them, Fireballs, strikes, combat text, a pause and resume
# and a settings save and reload every round, and a reset back to the start. Frame times over the whole run.
