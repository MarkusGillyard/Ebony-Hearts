# The Chapel wolves as the creator's "Chapel Wolves" document describes them (2026-11-23; development build: test
# positions, the alignment selector, the Shadow item, the pack's size). Alignment tiers: 0 Hero of Humanity,
# 2 Kindhearted, 4 Platonic, 7 Malevolent. Covers: life at rest (lying down, asleep, lifting its head as someone
# passes); an evil aura warned before any fight and free to leave, and coming too close starting it; the pack judging
# together; harm to one wolf bringing the others; prayer as sanctuary (no wolf attacks the one praying, whatever they
# are; the wolves stand guard and go for anything striking at them); a neutral player protected; a friend greeted on
# coming back and lain beside when badly hurt; talking with a friend; a Shadow told to pray, unable to cast, restored by
# three days of prayer, and exorcised for striking or for ignoring the warning.
wait 6
fp_expect screen eq 12
fp_expect alignment eq 4

log == at rest: with no one near it lies down; asleep, it lifts its head when someone passes
fp_reset
fp_teleport 0 300 0 -90 0
wait 1.0
fp_expect wolf_lying eq 1
fp_expect wolf_calm eq 1
fp_wolf_place 330 -1530 90
fp_wolf_rest 1
wait 0.5
fp_expect wolf_asleep eq 1
fp_teleport 330 -930 0 -90 0
wait 0.5
fp_expect wolf_asleep eq 0
fp_expect wolf_lying eq 1
fp_expect wolf_hostile eq 0

fp_expect health eq 100
