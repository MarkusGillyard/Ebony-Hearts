# The Chapel wolf in the Shipping package: device inputs and observation only (no teleports, no alignment selector,
# no stat changes). The player starts Platonic (Neutral): the wolf rests at the right of the altar end and keeps its
# distance as the player walks down the aisle; a jab into the air well away from it is no threat; the pause menu's
# Reset the wolf puts it back at its rest; walking out to the entrance strip leaves it calm. The bands, harm, the
# Hero bond and the Ice Wall rerouting are covered in the development build (fp_wolf.txt).
wait 5
fp_expect screen eq 12
fp_expect alignment eq 4
fp_expect wolf_state eq 0
fp_expect wolf_hostile eq 0
fp_expect wolf_home_dist lt 60

log == down the aisle past the dummy: the wolf stays calm and keeps its distance
hold kbA FPLeft
wait 0.7
release kbA FPLeft
hold kbA FPForward
wait 3.5
release kbA FPForward
wait 1.0
fp_state aisle
fp_expect y lt -400
fp_expect wolf_hostile eq 0
fp_expect wolf_provoked eq 0
fp_expect wolf_dist gt 380
fp_expect health eq 100

log == a jab into the air far from it is no threat
press kbA FPJab
wait 0.8
fp_expect wolf_disengages eq 0
fp_expect wolf_provoked eq 0
fp_expect alignment eq 4

log == pause, Reset the wolf: back at its rest, calm
press kbA Pause
wait 0.2
press kbA Down
press kbA Down
press kbA Down
press kbA Down
press kbA Down
press kbA Confirm
wait 0.5
fp_expect paused eq 0
fp_expect wolf_state eq 0
fp_expect wolf_home_dist lt 60
fp_expect wolf_health eq 60

log == back out to the entrance strip: still calm
hold kbA FPBack
wait 4.0
release kbA FPBack
wait 1.0
fp_state entrance
fp_expect wolf_hostile eq 0
fp_expect alignment eq 4
fp_expect wolf_dead eq 0
quit
