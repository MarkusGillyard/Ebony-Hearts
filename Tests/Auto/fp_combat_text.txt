# Comic-book combat text (milestone 7; development build: test positions, the alignment, the pack's size, forced
# lines). Covers: the player's lines; lines arising on their own from a real four-wolf fight (occasional, never more
# than three at once, never overlapping, inside the view); several speakers side by side; expiry; the jump-out and
# reduced flashing; the interface scale; a speaker off screen; a neutral wolf's warning and a friendly wolf's greeting
# arising on their own; a reset clearing every line. Lines are the marked placeholders in firstperson.json.
wait 5
fp_expect screen eq 12
fp_expect ct_visible eq 0

log == the player answers a blow (the first line of its kind always comes)

wait 0.2
fp_expect ct_player eq 1

fp_expect ct_placeholders eq 1

wait 4
fp_expect ct_visible eq 0

log == a real fight: four evil wolves, twenty seconds; lines come by themselves, at most three, never overlapping
fp_stat health 3000

fp_wolves 4
fp_align 7
fp_teleport 0 -900 0 -90 0
fp_text_stats
wait 6
screenshot ct_fight_a
wait 6

fp_expect ct_hostile gt 2
fp_expect ct_max_visible lt 4
fp_expect ct_overlaps eq 0
fp_expect ct_outside eq 0
fp_expect ct_refused gt 0
