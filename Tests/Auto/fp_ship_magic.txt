# The spells in the Shipping package (the F10 check expects no developer panel; a development build opens it): device inputs and observation only (no teleports, no stat changes).
# From the start the player casts at the training dummy 4.5 m ahead: Fireball, Ice Wall with its freeze, the wall
# blocking the way, Fireball melting it, a cast refused without the Qhi, the controller, F10 (no panel in Shipping),
# pause mid-cast and reset. Also the review footage's script: record with capture.py --start-on
# <evidence>/auto_log.txt:review_start.
wait 5
fp_expect screen eq 12
fp_expect qhi eq 100
log review_start
wait 1.0

log == Fireball at the dummy: 20 Qhi, 25 damage
press kbA FPFireball
wait 1.2
fp_expect fireball_hits eq 1
fp_expect dummy_damage eq 25
fp_expect qhi lt 90
fp_dummy_reset
wait 1.0

log == Ice Wall: it rises across the view, freezes the water and catches the dummy's feet
press kbA FPIceWall
wait 0.6
fp_expect walls eq 1
fp_expect wall_ice_water eq 1
fp_expect dummy_frozen eq 1
log walking into it stops at the ice
hold kbA FPForward
wait 1.2
release kbA FPForward
fp_expect y gt 130
wait 0.8

log == Fireball melts it
press kbA FPFireball
wait 1.2
fp_expect walls eq 0
fp_expect walls_melted eq 1
hold kbA FPForward
wait 0.6
release kbA FPForward
fp_expect y lt 120
wait 0.5

log == Qhi runs short: Fireballs in a row until one is refused, with nothing spent
press kbA FPFireball
wait 0.5
press kbA FPFireball
wait 0.5
press kbA FPFireball
wait 0.5
fp_expect qhi lt 20
press kbA FPFireball
wait 0.3
fp_expect refused_qhi gt 0
fp_expect qhi lt 20
fp_dummy_reset
wait 1.0

log == strikes feed Qhi: jab, straight, then a Fireball straight after
press kbA FPJab
wait 0.25
press kbA FPStraight
wait 0.7
fp_expect dummy_hits eq 2
fp_dummy_reset
wait 3.0
press kbA FPShove
wait 0.25
press kbA FPFireball
wait 1.2
fp_dummy_reset
wait 1.0

log == controller: L2 / LT Fireball, Triangle / Y Ice Wall
fp_reset
wait 1.0
axis pad7 Gamepad_LeftTriggerAxis 1.0
wait 0.1
axis pad7 Gamepad_LeftTriggerAxis 0.0
wait 1.2
fp_expect fireball_hits eq 1
fp_dummy_reset
wait 0.8
press pad7 FPIceWall
wait 1.5
fp_expect walls eq 1
log review_end

log == F10 opens nothing in Shipping (the developer panel exists only in development builds)
fp_devkey
wait 0.3
fp_expect paused eq 0
fp_expect dev_panel eq 0

log == pause mid-cast, then Return to the start clears every spell
press kbA FPFireball
wait 0.1
press kbA Pause
fp_expect action eq 4
press kbA Down
press kbA Confirm
wait 0.4
fp_expect action eq 0
fp_expect walls eq 0
fp_expect fireballs eq 0
fp_expect qhi eq 100
fp_expect dummy_frozen eq 0
# Footage capture runs slower than real time; the window stays open until it has every frame.
wait 12
quit
