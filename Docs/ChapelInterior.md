# The Drowned Chapel: interior

The Drowned Chapel is the game's sanctuary and hub. This document covers the playable interior on the
`proto/chapel-interior` branch. Its design (the spaces, their purposes and the decisions awaiting the creator) is a
proposal until the creator approves it. Story details are kept out of this public document.

Everything built here is a placeholder. The pieces are generated from code with the Chapel's own geometry helpers and
materials, so they read as the same building; the creator authors the final architecture and art.

## Spaces

Coordinates are Unreal centimetres. The nave runs from the entrance (+Y) to the rose window (−Y), and X runs across
it.

| Space | Purpose | Level | Stage 1 |
|---|---|---|---|

## What changed in the map

`Tools/Unreal/build_chapel_interior.py` makes these changes:
- It removes the grey west-wall placeholder.
- It removes four aisle wall bays (where the transepts open) and the north aisle's east end bay (the way down),
  with their glass, candles and grime decals.
- It places the new pieces with collision from their render geometry and two-sided shadows.
- It adds eleven lights:
  - warm in the hall, transepts and by the great arch;
  - the forge's (the only new shadowed light besides the door seam's);
  - cold in the way down and the crypt;
  - a faint red at the gate and a faint warmth at the sealed door.

Re-running it replaces its own actors (labels `IX_*`, `IXL_*`). The bays it replaced are recorded in the source folder
(`bays.json`), so a re-run can place the arches without the original walls.

## Water

The Chapel's water is now a set of sheets, each over its own area: the nave's basin at the tuned surface (3 cm) and
the crypt's at its mesh's height (−2.4 m). The player's wading depth, the Ice Wall's freezing and the ripples all
read the sheet under them. The Threshold Hall, the loft, the transepts and the stair are dry.

## Navigation

The wolves walk everywhere the player can. The navigation bounds now cover the whole interior, and the new rooms'
floors, every stair step and the wall stubs beside each opening have navigation-only stand-ins
(`Tools/Unreal/adapt_chapel_fp.py`, `IX_NAV`). A friendly wolf follows the player into the forge, up to the loft and
down into the crypt.

## Rebuilding

```bash
blender -b --factory-startup -P Tools/Blender/chapel/chapel_interior.py -- <workspace>/sourceart/chapel_interior
bin/editor_py.sh Tools/Unreal/build_chapel_interior.py
EH_FP_STAGE=nav bin/editor_py.sh Tools/Unreal/adapt_chapel_fp.py
```

(`CHAPEL_SLICE_ROOT` points the Blender script at the slice root, for the kit's configuration.)

## Tests

`Tests/Auto/fp_interior.txt` (development build) walks every new space with real movement:
- the hall, where the door holds;
- the stair up to the loft and out onto the balcony;
- both transepts' steps;
- the way down into the crypt's water, and back up;
- the gate and the sealed door, which hold;
- a friendly wolf following the player into the forge, the loft and the crypt.

It checks the water depth in each place.

`Tests/Auto/fp_perf_interior.txt` (all builds, inputs only) is a warm frame-time route through the hall, up the stair,
onto the balcony, through both transepts and down into the crypt. It is generated by `Tests/Auto/gen_perf_interior.py`.

The stair's parapet starts three steps up, at a newel post, so the stair can be stepped onto from the side at its foot.

## Stage 2: function and furnishing

- **Interact** (E, Circle / B; settings format 10).
  - What the player looks at within 2.3 m, and in plain sight, shows a prompt.
  - Using it shows a short placeholder text, marked as such. The text is data (`Content/Data/firstperson.json`,
    `chapel`), so the creator can rewrite it.
  - Interactables: the Threshold Door, the forge's counter (buying) and anvil (upgrading) and Dwar's stand-in, the
    cave gate and the sealed door. None of them opens or sells anything yet.
- **Prayer** at the sanctuary's kneeler.
  - Interact kneels, and the view turns, unhurried, to the altar and the rose window.
  - Walking, techniques and spells stop, and looking is slowed.
  - The alignment meter shows: nine steps from white (good) through grey to black (evil), with a mark at the player's
    tier and no names. It is the only place the meter can be seen.
  - Interact, a step, a jump or a strike rises. The press that rises only rises; for a quarter second nothing else
    starts.
  - A reset or death ends the prayer.
  - Time running faster during prayer waits for the world clock (stage 3).
- **Furnishings** (`Tools/Blender/chapel/chapel_furnish.py`, placed by `Tools/Unreal/build_chapel_furnish.py`):
  - **Sanctuary:**
    - the kneeler: a kneeling board, and a desk carrying a cracked heart;
    - a white-to-black inlay of nine stones at the platform's edge;
    - one veiled statue behind the altar, beneath the rose window.
  - **Forge:**
    - a hearth with a stone hood and embers, and a bellows;
    - an anvil on a stump, a quench trough, a counter with goods, and a rack of plain blades;
    - Dwar's stand-in, a plain figure in the placeholder grid material.
  - **Hall of the Six:**
    - six alcoves with candles; five hold a trace each, and one is empty;
    - a blank framed panel on the end wall: a slot for the creator's illustration.
  - **The nave:** two blank devotional boards on the piers (illustration slots).
  - **The camp in the south aisle:** bedrolls, a brazier with embers and its own small light, crates, a sack and a
    pot.
  - **New material instances:** cloth tints, plaster, an ember glow, faintly lime-green water and a red flare.
  - **Navigation:** the furniture has navigation stand-ins, so the wolves walk round it.

## Stage 3 (first part): atmosphere

- **Placed sounds.** Each room has its own looping sound, placed where it comes from: the forge's fire, the camp's
  brazier, and the crypt's low tone and lapping water. They are synthesized placeholders
  (`Tools/Audio/eh_audio/ambience.py`), follow the Ambience volume setting, and fall off with distance. Their places
  and volumes are data (`chapel.sounds`).
- **The Hall of the Six stays silent**, by design.
- **The forge's embers.** Embers rise off the coals now and then while the player is near (`chapel.sparkAt`,
  `sparkRange`, `sparkGap`).
- **The camp's brazier light** is brighter, so the camp reads.
- **Not built:** the light following a world clock. A clock is a new system and awaits the creator's go-ahead.
