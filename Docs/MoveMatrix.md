# Move interaction matrix

What each move leads into, measured by running the combat core on the shipped data (`Content/Data`). The generated
tables below cover every ground source a fighter starts offense with and every ground follow-up, on hit and on block.
Defences against each kind of hit are in the [Combat contract](CombatContract.md) (hit levels) and are checked by
`Tests/Core` (`guard_heights_lows_overheads_and_jump_ins`, `hit_levels_agree_with_the_crouching_hurtboxes`). Jump-ins are
covered by `jump_in_reward_depends_on_height` and `deep_jump_in_links_into_ground_offense`; the bounded-route searches
by `no_infinite_or_unbounded_combo_search`, `jump_ins_cannot_loop` and `throw_and_uppercut_loops_are_bounded`.

How to read the dispositions:
- **combo** (a cancel) or **link combo**: a true combo; the defender never gets an actionable frame.
- **frame trap** (on block): the follow-up hits a defender who never had a free frame to act after the block; it is
  beaten only by guarding at the right height for the follow-up.
- **blockstring**: the follow-up is blocked without a gap.
- **gap N**: pressure with a hole: the defender had N free frames. Faster options (a jab is 8, the uppercut 9-10, a
  throw 5, the rolls invulnerable from 3-4) can take the turn; at a gap over the follow-up's startup the follow-up is a
  punishable commitment.
- **x**: no route (no cancel, and nothing came out in the window). **whiffs**: out of reach (pushback).
- A knockdown (the uppercut, specials, the throws) ends the route: knockdowns are invulnerable, so nothing follows.

Notable dispositions:
- Down + Light during a light's cancel window continues the string (L2 / L3), because the low is not a cancel target:
  down does not break a string, and the low comes out only from neutral.
- The combo throw is a combo only from Light / Light, Light on hit at close range; on block the same press is pressure
  (gap 4 after a blocked jab: the opponent may jump, roll, strike or escape).
- After a blocked uppercut the defender has 30+ free frames: any move punishes it.
