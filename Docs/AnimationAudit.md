# Animation audit

Every clip the build exports, measured from the exported FBX (evidence/blocking-1109/anim-audit-cloth2; `Tools/Blender/anim/audit_clips.py`, regenerated by `Tools/Docs/anim_audit_table.py`). Units are cm per frame at 60 fps. *Fastest* is the largest single-frame step of a main joint (a fist or foot peaks near 25-35 cm/f); *pops* are steps over 15 cm more than 3x their neighbours; *held* is the share of frames with no joint moving 0.05 cm (frozen or duplicated samples show up here; deliberate holds are marked); *flips* are elbows or knees moving 20 cm and 2.5x both neighbours; *floor* is the lowest point of the body. A row is *checked* when nothing is flagged; flagged rows carry a disposition.

Runtime behaviour checked with these clips: samples are taken at the simulation frame plus the render alpha (`EHFighterVisual`), frozen during hitstop; transitions are inertial offsets from what was last drawn (`EHAnimInstance`), so a restart mid-transition stays continuous; facing flips cut without a blend by design. Display pacing (V-Sync at a refresh the compositor does not match) is a separate cause of uneven motion and is measured in `Docs/Validation.md`, not here.
